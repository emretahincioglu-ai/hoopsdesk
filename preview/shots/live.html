How it behaves
• Opens on LIVE: slider at the end, newest play on top.
• New play arrives → slides into the stream and the shot drops onto the court together (green dot = make, red X = miss, yellow ring flash).
• Drag back → court and stream show the game only up to that moment. New plays keep coming in the background; the button shows “+2 new” so you know.
• Tap LIVE → snaps back to now.
• After the game: same view, slider becomes a full-game replay.
