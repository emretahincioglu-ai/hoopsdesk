How it works
• One court for the whole matchup. Every shot goes on it where it was taken (your players gold, theirs purple), with a pop-up like “+3 Maxey”.
• Rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and turnovers flash across the top of the court, since they have no spot.
• The category bar on top updates live and lights up when a category flips (tap a category to see only those plays).
• Chips filter the court and feed: All, Mine, Theirs, or one player.
• No team direction or game clock like ESPN, since it's 10 different games. Each play shows its own game and time instead.
