Monday Setup · preview

Your real ANTI-QUEMO roster and the real NBA schedule for next week (Nov 2–8). One screen you go through once a week, about 2 minutes, before the projections run.

  1. Day strip: how many of your players play each day against your 10 starting spots. Red = players will sit on your bench, green = open spots to stream.
  2. Needs your call: only the players where a decision matters (day-to-day, injury management, back-to-backs). Tap the games he'll play.
  3. Rest rules: set once, applied every week (e.g. Embiid skips the 2nd night of a back-to-back).
  4. Everyone else: the full week grid. Tap any game to sit him. "BN" = he plays, but you have more players than spots that day, so he likely sits your bench.
  5. Opponent tab: same screen for their roster.

Once you save, every projection (matchup, win %, waiver picks) uses your calls instead of "everyone plays every game". Then we backtest: model alone vs model plus your picks.
